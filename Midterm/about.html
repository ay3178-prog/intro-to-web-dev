about me

Hi, I'm Aparna.

I'm a student in New York who collects languages the way some people collect postcards. I grew up with English and Hindi, picked up Spanish and Turkish along the way, and right now I'm a beginner all over again with Italian.

I started this blog because learning someone's language is how I show respect for where they come from. I want to share the awkward, funny, real moments so other learners feel a little less alone when it's their turn to speak.

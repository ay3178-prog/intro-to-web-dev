Hi, I'm Aparna.

I speak English, Hindi, Spanish and Turkish, and I'm learning Italian right now. This blog is about what it actually feels like to speak a new language: the nerves, the mistakes, and the moments it finally clicks.

Why learn a language?

  • Respect

    Speaking someone's language shows you care where they come from.

  • Connection

    Nothing gets lost in translation when you talk in their mother tongue.

  • Confidence

    Every awkward first conversation makes the next one easier.

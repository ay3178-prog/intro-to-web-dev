My Favorite Pasta

Ingredients

  • 8 oz pasta
  • 2 tbsp olive oil
  • 3 cloves garlic
  • 1/2 cup parmesan
  • Salt and pepper

Instructions

  1. Boil the pasta until al dente.
  2. Heat olive oil and garlic in a pan.
  3. Add the cooked pasta.
  4. Stir in parmesan.
  5. Season with salt and pepper.

Notes

This is one of my favorite quick meals because it is simple and easy to customize.
